  FOR TESTING RISK LEVEL HARMLESS DURATION SHORT.

  PRIVATE SECTION.
    DATA test_instance TYPE REF TO zcldlt_codekata_tengreenbottle.

    METHODS setup.
    METHODS teardown.
    METHODS shall_be_ok        FOR TESTING RAISING cx_static_check cx_dynamic_check.
    METHODS shall_be_ok_with_9 FOR TESTING RAISING cx_static_check cx_dynamic_check.
    METHODS shall_fail_with_11 FOR TESTING RAISING cx_static_check cx_dynamic_check.
    METHODS shall_fail_with_0  FOR TESTING RAISING cx_static_check cx_dynamic_check.
ENDCLASS.
